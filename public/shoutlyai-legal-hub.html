Global Compliance
Regional compliance coverage
Shoutly AI is operated by Qubixel Technologies Private Limited and serves users globally. We maintain compliance with the primary data protection laws in each region.
European Union & EEA
GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679). Applies to all EU and EEA residents regardless of where processing occurs. Enforced by local Data Protection Authorities.
Compliant
United States — California
CCPA / CPRA
California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act. Applies to California residents. Covers right to know, right to delete, right to opt-out of sale, right to correct.
Compliant
India
DPDP Act 2023
Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. India’s primary data protection law. Shoutly AI operates as a Data Fiduciary. Applies to all Indian users. Grievance redressal within 30 days.
Compliant
United Kingdom
UK GDPR
Post-Brexit retained version of the GDPR under the UK Data Protection Act 2018. Largely mirrors GDPR requirements. ICO is the supervisory authority.
Compliant
United States — Federal
CAN-SPAM / COPPA
CAN-SPAM Act governs commercial email communications. COPPA governs data collection from users under 13. Shoutly AI does not collect data from or market to users under 18.
Compliant
Global — Content
DMCA
Digital Millennium Copyright Act. Shoutly AI follows DMCA safe harbor procedures for user-generated and AI-generated content. Takedown requests processed within 48 hours.
