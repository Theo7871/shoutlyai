Canva vs Shoutly AI FAQ

Straight answers without outdated claims about either platform.

Is Shoutly AI a Canva alternative?

Shoutly AI can be an alternative for businesses primarily looking for social media content creation, scheduling and publishing. It is not intended to replace Canva's broad graphic design, presentation, document, website and visual communication capabilities.

Is Canva better than Shoutly AI?

Canva is stronger as a general-purpose visual design platform. Shoutly AI is focused more narrowly on social media automation. The better option depends on whether your primary requirement is design flexibility or automated social publishing.

Does Canva have AI?

Yes. Canva has extensive AI capabilities through Canva AI, Magic Studio and newer AI experiences.

Can Canva schedule social media posts?

Yes. Canva provides a Social Content Planner that allows users to plan, schedule and publish content to supported social channels.

Can Shoutly AI replace Canva?

Not completely, and that is not the strongest positioning for Shoutly AI. Canva is a broad visual creation platform. Shoutly AI should instead be positioned as a specialized social media automation platform that can reduce the work involved in creating and publishing ongoing social content.

Which is better for social media?

If the requirement is creating highly customized designs manually, Canva has a major advantage. If the requirement is generating business-specific social content and moving it through a repeatable creation, calendar, scheduling and publishing workflow, Shoutly AI has a more specialized proposition.

Which is better for a small business?

A business that needs logos, flyers, presentations, videos, marketing collateral and social graphics may benefit more from Canva. A business primarily struggling to keep its social channels consistently populated may prefer Shoutly AI's specialized automation workflow.

Does Shoutly AI generate captions and hashtags?

Yes. Shoutly AI's product workflow includes generated social content with captions and hashtags.

Does Canva generate social media captions?

Yes. Canva's AI writing tools can assist with copywriting and social captions.

What is the biggest difference between Canva and Shoutly AI?

Canva is a broad visual communication and design platform that includes social media capabilities. Shoutly AI is built specifically around social media content automation including content generation, visuals, captions, hashtags, calendar, scheduling and publishing.
