What partners get

Everything you need to run client social media at scale.

The partner programme gives agencies and resellers the tools to manage multiple clients efficiently — without the overhead of managing multiple separate tools.

👥
Multi-client dashboard
Switch between client accounts from one login. Each client gets their own brand kit, content calendar, platform connections, and analytics. No separate accounts, no shared logins, no confusion.
✦
AI content generation for every client
The AI generates platform-specific content for each client in their brand voice, for all 10 platforms. You set up each client’s brand kit once — the AI applies it to every post automatically from that point forward.
🍳
Per-client brand kits
Each client gets their own brand kit — logo, colours, fonts, tone of voice, industry context. The AI uses this to generate content that sounds like each client, not a generic template. No mixing between clients.
📅
Approval workflows
Set up an approval step so your team or the client reviews AI-generated content before it publishes. Nothing goes live without sign-off. Clients can approve directly without needing full platform access.
📊
Unified analytics across clients
See performance metrics across all client accounts in one view, or drill into individual client dashboards. Impressions, engagement, reach, follower growth — all in one place for your monthly client reports.
💬
Dedicated partner support
Partners get direct access to the Shoutly AI team via partners@shoutlyai.com. Onboarding support, technical questions, and partnership discussions handled separately from standard customer support.

How the partner programme works

From application to managing your first client

A straightforward process from enquiry to onboarding. We keep it simple because we’re a small team and want partners who are serious about building.

1
Apply via the form or email us directly
Fill in the application form on this page or email partners@shoutlyai.com directly. Tell us about your agency or business, how many clients you currently manage, and what you’re looking to build on Shoutly AI. No deck or pitch required — a clear email is enough.
2
A conversation with the Shoutly AI team
We’ll schedule a call to understand your setup, how you work with clients, and what you need from the platform. This isn’t a sales call — it’s us figuring out if the fit is right and what a practical arrangement looks like for your scale.
3
Platform access and onboarding
Once agreed, you get platform access with multi-client setup, brand kits for each client, and a walkthrough of the approval and publishing workflows. Our team helps you set up the first two clients so the process is clear before you scale.
4
Manage clients and grow your capacity
Add clients to your dashboard, set up their brand kits, connect their social accounts, and let the AI handle daily content generation and scheduling. You focus on client relationships and strategy — Shoutly AI handles the execution.
